### What to Watch For

- Journey maps with only positive emotions — the pain points are the insight
- User flows with more than one purpose per screen
- MVP that includes everything — push the "if we only shipped one thing" test

### Facilitation Tips

- At journey mapping: "What emotion does the new hire have at this step?" stickies in 3 colours (positive/neutral/negative)
- For each screen in the flow: "What is the ONE thing this screen needs the user to do?"
- At debrief: map the emotional pain points to the 40% miss rate — are they correlated?

### Extension / Coaching Office Hours

Participants can continue extension work independently and bring it to **Coaching Office Hours**.
At Office Hours, focus on: what decision did they make, why, and what would they change?

### Your Starting State

You have:
- `research/hr-checklist.md` — the current 30-step email-based checklist
- `research/new-hire-survey-quotes.md` — 3 new hire survey responses

Your goal: map the current journey, design the future-state flow, and identify the MVP.

### Step by Step

**Step 1:** Map the current onboarding journey in Miro. Stages, touchpoints, emotions, pain points. One sticky per step.

**Step 2:** Design the future-state flow: 5 screens max. Each screen does one thing.

**Step 3:** Circle the MVP subset: if you only shipped one screen, which one solves the 40% miss rate?

### What Good Looks Like

A current-state journey with honestly negative emotions at the pain points. A future-state flow where each screen has a single, clear purpose.

See the `solutions/` directory for reference examples — but try the exercise first.

### Extension Work

- Map the manager's journey alongside the new hire's — where do they need to coordinate?
- Write 3 usability test tasks you'd run with a real new hire on your future-state flow
- Sketch the error states: what happens when a required step is missed?

Bring your extension work to **Coaching Office Hours**. You'll get 15 minutes of focused feedback.
