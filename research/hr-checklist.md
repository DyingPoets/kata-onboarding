# Employee Onboarding Checklist — Current State
**Format:** Email sent by HR on day 1
**Status:** 40% of new hires miss at least one critical step in week 1

---

## Before Day 1 (HR)
1. Send offer letter countersigned
2. Background check cleared
3. IT ticket raised for: laptop, email, system access
4. Notify facilities: desk assignment, building access card
5. Add to payroll

## Day 1 (New Hire — email-guided)
6. Report to reception at 9am
7. Collect building access card from reception
8. Meet with HR (30 min): paperwork, benefits enrollment, policy review
9. Collect laptop from IT
10. Attend IT setup session (1 hr)
11. Set up email and Slack
12. Complete I-9 form (must be done in person, day 1)
13. Review and sign employee handbook

## Week 1 (New Hire — self-directed)
14. Complete security training (online, 2 hrs)
15. Complete harassment prevention training (online, 1 hr)
16. Set up 1:1 with manager
17. Meet with team (manager arranges)
18. Complete payroll direct deposit setup (HR portal)
19. Enroll in health benefits (HR portal — 30-day window)
20. Get ID badge photo taken (facilities, 2nd floor)
21. Set up laptop with required software (IT wiki)
22. Request system access for: [varies by role]
23. Complete role-specific onboarding checklist (sent by manager separately)

## Week 2 (New Hire)
24. Complete any remaining compliance training
25. Submit expense report setup form (Finance)
26. Add emergency contact to HR system
27. Set up 401k contribution (HR portal)
28. Review and acknowledge data handling policy
29. Complete IT security awareness training
30. Manager confirms onboarding completion to HR

---

**Most Commonly Missed (per HR data):**
- Step 12 (I-9) — missed by 22% of hires (causes legal risk)
- Step 19 (benefits enrollment) — missed by 31% until close to 30-day deadline
- Step 22 (system access request) — missed by 18% (delays productive work by days)
