# New Hire Survey — Selected Responses
**Survey period:** Last 6 months (n=28 respondents)

---

**Quote 1 — Engineering hire, week 3:**
"I got the email checklist on day 1 and I genuinely didn't know what to do first or what was urgent vs. just eventually. I thought the I-9 was optional because it was buried in the middle. My manager didn't know about it either. HR had to call me on day 3."

---

**Quote 2 — Marketing hire, week 6:**
"Benefits enrollment was the one that stressed me out the most. I knew there was a 30-day window but I kept putting it off because the portal was confusing and I didn't know which plan to pick. I finally did it on day 28 at 11pm. I almost lost it."

---

**Quote 3 — Sales hire, month 2:**
"Honestly the worst part was not knowing if I was doing the right things in the right order. The checklist had 30 items and no indication of what happened if I missed something. What's mandatory? What's just nice to have? Nobody told me. I just guessed."
